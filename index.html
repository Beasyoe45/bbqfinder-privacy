🔥 BBQ Finder

Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated: April 1, 2026

BBQ Finder does not collect, sell, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

BBQ Finder does not collect any personal information. The app operates entirely on your device. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • We do not collect your location data (location features use your device's built-in GPS only, and no location data is transmitted to us)
  • We do not collect usage analytics or behavioral data
  • We do not use advertising networks or tracking tools
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles

2. Data Stored on Your Device

BBQ Finder stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Reviews — Star ratings and comments you write are saved locally on your device
  • Favorites — Restaurants you mark as favorites are saved locally on your device
  • Restaurant photos — Any photos you upload for restaurants are saved locally on your device
  • App preferences — Settings like dark mode are saved locally on your device

None of this data is transmitted to any server. It lives only on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the app.

3. Third-Party Services

BBQ Finder uses the following third-party services:

  • OpenStreetMap — Used to display the interactive map. OpenStreetMap may collect standard server logs when map tiles are loaded. See OpenStreetMap's privacy policy.
  • Unsplash — Used to display food photos for restaurants. Unsplash may collect standard server logs when images are loaded. See Unsplash's privacy policy.
  • Google Maps — Used only when you tap "Directions," which opens Google Maps. See Google's privacy policy.

4. Permissions

BBQ Finder may request the following device permissions:

  • Internet access — Required to load map tiles and restaurant photos
  • Camera / Photo library — Only if you choose to upload a restaurant photo. Photos are stored locally only

5. Children's Privacy

BBQ Finder does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app contains no content inappropriate for children and does not target children specifically.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Your Rights

Since BBQ Finder stores no personal data on any server, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete on our end. You can delete all locally stored app data at any time by uninstalling BBQ Finder from your device.
